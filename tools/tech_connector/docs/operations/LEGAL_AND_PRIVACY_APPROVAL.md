# Legal and Privacy Approval

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Retain qualified software,
commercial, privacy, and tax counsel in every launch jurisdiction.

Counsel must review the repository-root notice, `tech_connector/LICENSE.md`,
`PRIVACY.md`, the Community project terms, pricing/upgrade/refund language,
Enterprise order form, data-processing terms, and the exact account-portal
acceptance screen. The review must explicitly address source availability,
redistribution, reverse engineering and mandatory local-law exceptions,
commercial use, the USD $500,000 lifetime Adjusted Project Profit threshold,
eligible-cost and anti-avoidance language, reporting/audit rights, termination,
existing-project treatment after a terms change, consumer law, tax, sanctions,
minors, data retention, deletion, and subprocessors.

Acceptance evidence must include an opaque account ID, agreement ID/version,
content SHA-256, acceptance time, method, and relevant project/organization ID.
Do not use a pre-checked box. The portal must display or directly link the exact
accepted text and make historical acceptances visible to the user and authorized
administrators. Existing accepted project terms are immutable; new terms receive
a new ID and version.

Verification evidence is a protected counsel approval/matter reference plus the
approved document hashes. After approval, change the notice’s release status only
to the exact wording required by the production gate and record the evidence
reference in `production_readiness.json`.
