# Contributing to Tech Connector

Community involvement is welcome. The goal is to make high-end creative,
pipeline, DCC, and automation tooling more accessible while keeping the project
sustainable.

## What Contributions Mean

By submitting code, documentation, examples, designs, issues, comments, tests,
or other contributions, you agree to the contribution terms in
[LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

In plain language:

- you keep ownership of your contribution;
- you grant The Entire World, LLC permission to use, modify, distribute,
  commercialize, and relicense that contribution as part of Tech Connector and
  related products;
- your contribution may appear in free community releases, paid commercial
  releases, enterprise builds, or internal tools;
- you should only submit work you have the right to contribute.

This is what allows community edits to be merged without blocking future
commercial licensing.

## What Contributions Do Not Allow

Contributing does not give anyone the right to resell Tech Connector.

You may not use contributions or modified versions to sell, sublicense,
repackage, host, redistribute, or commercialize Tech Connector itself unless The
Entire World, LLC grants a separate written commercial license.

## Good Contributions

Good contributions usually include:

- bug fixes with a focused test or validation note;
- improvements to Maya, Unreal, Blender, or other DCC bridge behavior;
- safer pipeline-node generation and dependency discovery;
- clearer user-facing documentation;
- workflow examples that do not include private/proprietary assets;
- small, reviewable changes that match existing project patterns.

## Third-Party Code and Assets

Do not submit third-party code, model weights, plugins, assets, SDK files, or
generated content unless you have confirmed that the license permits inclusion
under Tech Connector's contribution terms.

If a contribution depends on an external project, include:

- the project name;
- the source URL;
- the license;
- why it is needed;
- whether it is optional or required.

## Private Data

Do not submit private user data, proprietary production files, credentials,
tokens, unreleased intellectual property, client files, or restricted assets.

## Review Philosophy

The project favors practical changes that make the tools more capable, safer,
and easier to understand. For behavior changes, include enough detail that a
reviewer can reproduce the issue or validate the improvement.
