# Tech Connector Indie Named-User License Terms

Release status: pre-release draft — qualified legal and tax review required

Terms version: TC-INDIE-2026.1

These terms supplement the Tech Connector Community Source License for an Indie
offer accepted through an official account. They are not an active offer until
the account portal displays an approved immutable offer ID, price, covered
version or subscription period, capabilities, and agreement content hash.

## 1. Eligibility and named users

The Indie path is intended for an individual or organization of 25 or fewer
people that is not performing major-corporate-IP work requiring an Enterprise
agreement. Employees and regularly engaged contractors across affiliated
entities count toward eligibility. Artificial entity splitting or temporary
staffing changes do not preserve Indie eligibility.

Each seat is assigned to one verified human account. Credentials and seats may
not be shared. A customer may administer named-user assignments through its
organization account subject to the accepted reassignment policy.

## 2. Draft standard offers

- Annual: USD $200 per named user for one year. An active annual entitlement
  covers releases made available during the paid term.
- Perpetual: USD $500 per named user for Tech Connector major version 6,
  including minor and patch updates through the final v6 release.
- Optional upgrade: USD $200 per named user to acquire major version 7 when
  offered. Declining an upgrade does not terminate the perpetual v6 grant.

Prices exclude applicable taxes and remain drafts until approved in the
versioned backend catalog. Regional pricing, renewals, cancellation, refunds,
tax treatment, and payment timing must be shown before purchase.

## 3. No project residual for paid covered use

No residual or profit share is owed for project work created under a valid paid
Indie entitlement using a version and capabilities covered by that entitlement.
The annual entitlement must be active when the licensed use occurs. A perpetual
entitlement continues for its covered major version even if support ends.

Use outside the covered version, named users, capabilities, organization,
eligibility limits, hosted-use rights, redistribution rights, or other grant
boundaries requires another approved offer or written agreement.

## 4. Devices, offline use, and support

The draft standard allowance is two registered devices per named user, with
self-service deactivation and replacement. A signed entitlement may operate
offline for up to 60 days, capped by the subscription or license status, before
online revalidation is required. Device identifiers are pseudonymous hashes;
project assets and creative content are not licensing metadata.

Standard self-service support is included during an active annual term. A
perpetual purchase does not include future major versions or indefinite support.
Any enhanced support commitment must be written in the accepted offer or a
separate agreement.

## 5. Source, tools, and restrictions

Public source visibility does not grant an activated entitlement. The signed
offer lists the permitted product capabilities. The complete Official Tools
Bundle is a single optional capability and is not included unless the accepted
offer expressly lists `official_tools_bundle`.

The redistribution, resale, hosted-service, sublicensing, circumvention, AI
training, and other restrictions in the controlling source license continue to
apply unless a signed agreement expressly changes them. The customer owns its
outputs and project assets.

## 6. Records and acceptance

The backend stores the immutable agreement ID and SHA-256, offer ID and version,
price terms, covered versions, capabilities, named user or organization, seat
assignment, device allowance, support period, and UTC acceptance time. Later
catalog changes do not rewrite an accepted paid grant.

This draft is not legal, tax, or accounting advice and is not approved for sale.
Counsel and tax professionals must approve consumer/business classification,
refund and renewal terms, supported jurisdictions, tax handling, governing law,
liability, warranty, and enforcement language before its release status changes
to `approved` or an Indie offer is published.
