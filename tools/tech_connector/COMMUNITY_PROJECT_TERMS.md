# Tech Connector Community Commercial Project Terms

Release status: pre-release draft — qualified legal and tax review required

Terms version: TC-COMMUNITY-PROJECT-2026.1

These project terms supplement the Tech Connector Community Source License for
a registered commercial project. They are not active for a project until the
official account portal displays the applicable version, studio-size band,
threshold, and marginal schedule and records acceptance by a verified account.
If these terms conflict with an individually signed Custom or Enterprise
agreement, that signed agreement controls to the extent of the conflict.

## 1. Registered project and accepted schedule

The acceptance record identifies the opaque Tech Connector project ID, account,
license, terms version, agreement content hash, acceptance time, classification
version, studio-size band, threshold, and marginal residual schedule. A project
path, project name, source file, scene, asset, animation, or other creative
content is not part of the registration record and must not be uploaded for
licensing purposes.

The studio-size band includes employees and regularly engaged contractors
working through the accepting person, organization, and affiliated entities.
Artificial entity splitting, temporary staffing changes, project splitting, or
transfers among related entities do not reduce the applicable band. A disputed
or changed classification must be resolved through a new recorded agreement or
written Custom terms; it must not silently rewrite an accepted project record.

## 2. Lifetime threshold and calculation basis

No residual is owed on the first USD $500,000 of cumulative Adjusted Project
Profit over the registered project's entire lifetime. Only Adjusted Project
Profit above that threshold is included in the marginal residual calculation.
Reaching a higher bracket never reprices profit in a lower bracket.

"Project Receipts," "Eligible Project Costs," and "Adjusted Project Profit"
have the meanings in the controlling Tech Connector license. Project Receipts
include amounts received or receivable from exploitation of the registered
project, less documented refunds, chargebacks, and taxes collected for a taxing
authority, as specified by the accepted schedule.

## 3. True Eligible Project Costs

An Eligible Project Cost must be actually paid or incurred, supported by
contemporaneous records, ordinary and necessary for the project, reasonable in
amount, and directly and primarily attributable to creating, producing,
distributing, supporting, or marketing the registered project.

Reasonably allocated shared costs count only when the allocation is documented,
consistent, proportionate, and tied to genuine project use. A payment to an
owner, affiliate, or related party counts only up to the lower of actual cost or
an arm's-length fair-market amount for genuine goods or services. Actual,
reasonable compensation for genuine owner or founder work may count. Unpaid
owner or founder time counts only under a pre-agreed capped allowance supported
by contemporaneous time records; it cannot be invented or repriced after the
project succeeds.

The following do not reduce Adjusted Project Profit: owner draws, dividends,
profit distributions, personal or unrelated expenses, excessive general
overhead, artificial management fees, unsupported corporate allocations,
inflated related-party charges, entity-level income taxes, financing costs not
directly attributable to the project, fines, penalties, reimbursed or
double-counted costs, costs already attributed to another project, non-economic
accounting allocations, and transactions whose principal purpose is avoiding a
threshold or residual.

## 4. Draft marginal schedules

The following launch schedules remain drafts until approved and published in
the account portal. Each percentage applies only within its stated lifetime
Adjusted Project Profit bracket.

| Lifetime Adjusted Project Profit | Solo | Micro, 2–5 people | Small, 6–25 people |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First $500,000 | 0% | 0% | 0% |
| $500,000–$1,000,000 | 0.50% | 0.75% | 1.00% |
| $1,000,000–$5,000,000 | 0.75% | 1.00% | 1.25% |
| Above $5,000,000 | 1.00% | 1.25% | 1.50% |

Organizations above 25 people, major corporate intellectual-property work,
hosted services, redistribution, and rights outside the Community grant require
an Enterprise or Custom agreement.

## 5. Reporting and records

The draft reporting period is annual after the project first exceeds the
threshold. The final accepted schedule controls reporting frequency, due dates,
payment mechanics, currency treatment, refunds, audit limits, record retention,
and dispute procedures. Reporting is limited to relevant financial records and
project-identifying metadata. The Entire World must not require project assets,
scenes, source files, animation data, or other creative content as residual
evidence.

Each report must use the same lifetime project scope and accepted cost standard.
Substantially identical editions, ports, releases, or components may be treated
as one project when the controlling license or accepted schedule requires it.

## 6. Versioning and acceptance evidence

The backend stores the immutable agreement ID, content SHA-256, classification
version, selected band, threshold, brackets, project ID, account or organization,
license ID, acceptance method, and UTC acceptance time. Later catalog changes do
not alter an already accepted project schedule. A replacement schedule requires
new review and acceptance and must preserve the prior acceptance history.
