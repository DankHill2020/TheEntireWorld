# Tech Connector Enterprise Named-User License Terms

Release status: pre-release draft — qualified legal, security, and tax review required

Terms version: TC-ENTERPRISE-2026.1

These terms are a draft standard framework for an organization order form. No
Enterprise rights, service level, integration, redistribution, hosted use, or
support commitment exists unless it appears in an approved offer and executed
order. Negotiated Custom terms control over this framework where they conflict.

## 1. Organization and named-user model

The license is granted to the identified organization. Each seat is assigned to
one verified human account that remains an active organization member. Seats and
credentials may not be shared. Organization owners or administrators manage
assignment and reason-coded reassignment through audited controls.

The draft allowance is two registered devices per named user and a 30-day
offline revalidation window. Air-gapped, build-agent, service-account, hosted,
or non-human automation rights require explicit Custom terms.

## 2. Draft standard pricing framework

- Annual: USD $1,500 per named user per year, five-seat minimum.
- Volume annual: USD $1,250 per named user at 25 or more seats; larger or
  nonstandard deployments are negotiated rather than automatically discounted.
- Perpetual: USD $3,500 per named user for Tech Connector major version 6,
  five-seat minimum, including minor and patch updates for v6.
- Optional perpetual support and major-version update plan: 20% of the license
  price annually when included in the order.

These figures are planning terms, not a public quote. The signed order controls
quantity, currency, taxes, invoicing, payment, renewal, support, price protection,
and any volume or founder discount.

## 3. Grant and project economics

The approved entitlement identifies covered versions and capabilities. Standard
paid Enterprise use carries no project residual or profit share unless the
signed order expressly adopts a success-based Custom structure. The customer
owns its outputs, project assets, and proprietary content.

Enterprise integrations, deployment assistance, source adaptation, SSO,
centralized administration, hosted use, redistribution, service/build-agent
rights, and the complete Official Tools Bundle are separately scoped. Public
source visibility does not include any capability absent from the signed
entitlement.

## 4. Support, security, and data

Only support hours, response targets, service levels, service credits, and
escalation paths written in the order are commitments. No 24/7 support or SLA is
implied. Security questionnaires, data-processing terms, incident notification,
retention, access controls, and enterprise integrations require written scope.

Licensing metadata is limited to identity, organization membership, agreement,
license, offer, seats, pseudonymous devices, project registration, and audit
records needed to administer the grant. Project assets, source files, scenes,
animation, and other creative content are not sent to the licensing service.

## 5. Restrictions and custom changes

The redistribution, resale, sublicensing, hosted-service, circumvention, AI
training, and other restrictions in the controlling source license continue to
apply except where the executed order expressly grants a specific right. Any
Custom term must be versioned and stored with the license rather than inferred
from a client-side tier name.

## 6. Records and approval

The backend stores the immutable agreement and order references, content hashes,
offer and version, organization, named seats, capabilities, covered versions,
support term, device rules, and acceptance evidence. Material changes require a
new agreement or order and preserve the prior history.

This draft is not legal, tax, security, or accounting advice and is not approved
for sale. Qualified counsel and the relevant tax and security professionals must
approve the final order form, liability and warranty terms, privacy and data
processing provisions, tax treatment, service commitments, governing law, and
enforcement language before its release status changes to `approved` or an
Enterprise offer is published.
