# Security Policy

## Supported releases

Security fixes are prioritized for the latest official signed Tech Connector
release and the current `main` branch. Older releases, forks, source-modified
builds, and unofficial packages may receive guidance, but are not guaranteed a
backport. License or support status never prevents responsible disclosure of a
security issue.

## Report a vulnerability privately

Do **not** post exploitable details, credentials, tokens, private keys, personal
data, or an unpatched proof of concept in a public GitHub issue or discussion.

Use the authenticated
[Tech Connector account portal](https://license.theentireworld.net/ui/portal.html),
open **Self-service support**, and select **Security report**. If the account or
sign-in flow itself is the affected component, use the contact method published
at [theentireworld.net](https://theentireworld.net/) and put `Security report`
in the subject or first line.

Include only what is needed to reproduce the issue:

- affected version, operating system, and host application;
- affected URL, component, or feature;
- concise reproduction steps and observed impact;
- whether the issue is already public or actively exploited; and
- a safe way to contact you.

Do not send project assets, scenes, source files, animation, renders, customer
records, or other creative work. Redact tokens, secrets, account identifiers,
device identifiers, and personal data. If a minimal proof of concept is needed,
wait for a private response before transferring it.

## What to expect

The pre-revenue response target is acknowledgement within five business days
and a triage update within ten business days. These are best-effort targets, not
a contractual SLA. Reports indicating active exploitation, key compromise,
authentication bypass, entitlement forgery, remote code execution, or exposure
of customer identity data are treated as urgent.

Please allow reasonable time to investigate and release a fix before public
disclosure. We will preserve reporter credit when requested and legally
permitted. This policy does not authorize privacy violations, destructive
testing, service disruption, social engineering, credential attacks, access to
other users' data, or testing against systems you do not own or have permission
to use.
